# ResponsibleSteel Global Steel Decarbonisation: From Complexity to Coherence Report 2026

## Overview
ResponsibleSteel's September 2026 report examines how fragmented policy frameworks worldwide are creating barriers to the transition to low-emission steelmaking. Steel accounts for around 8% of global greenhouse gas emissions, making its decarbonisation critical to climate goals. The report provides a global review with particular focus on the EU and India, highlighting trade measures, sustainable finance, public procurement, green steel labelling, and ESG regulations. [ResponsibleSteel News](https://www.responsiblesteel.org/news)

## Key Findings
- Policy fragmentation slows investment and the pace of decarbonisation.
- Greater alignment across governments is achievable and essential to unlock capital and deliver the transition.
- Leading steel producers and consumers (EU, India) illustrate both challenges and opportunities in policy design.

## Recommendations
The report calls for coherent policy packages that balance emissions reduction with industrial competitiveness, jobs, and capacity. It supports standards like ResponsibleSteel certification to provide reliable emissions data for procurement.

## Relevance to Industrial Decarbonization
This analysis complements existing steel pathways coverage by focusing on the policy coherence needed to scale solutions like hydrogen DRI and CCUS. It underscores the role of international coordination in hard-to-abate sectors.
