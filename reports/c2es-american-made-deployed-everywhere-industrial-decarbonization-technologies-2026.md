# C2ES American Made, Deployed Everywhere: U.S. Opportunities in Industrial Decarbonization Technologies Report 2026

**Source**: Center for Climate and Energy Solutions (C2ES), September 2026. [Executive Summary](https://www.c2es.org/industrial-markets-initiative/)

## Overview

The report analyzes U.S. opportunities in six priority industrial decarbonization technologies for process heat: advanced process controls/AI and digitalization, high-temperature industrial heat pumps, electric boilers, thermal energy storage (TES), electric furnaces, and industrial heat pumps/electrification solutions. It quantifies obtainable markets, potential U.S. revenue, employment, emissions reductions, and policy conditions for domestic capture versus imports.

## Key Findings

- Across the six technologies, the overlap-adjusted world serviceable obtainable market (SOM) reaches approximately $9 billion cumulatively by 2030 and $67 billion by 2040.
- By 2060, the world SOM reaches ~$463 billion cumulatively (~$13 billion per year average).
- U.S. SOM by 2060 is ~$73 billion; capturing it supports ~$82 billion in U.S. revenue (2026–2060), or ~$2.3 billion per year on average.
- Deployment and exports could create significant U.S. jobs while advancing emissions reductions.
- Policy support for manufacturing, deployment, and lead markets is critical to realizing domestic value.

## Relevance to Industrial Decarbonization

This complements existing U.S. DOE funding reports and state policies in the repository by providing a market and competitiveness lens on process heat electrification and related technologies. It highlights opportunities in sectors like chemicals, refining, and manufacturing already covered in topics/chemical-industry-decarbonization.md and topics/refining-decarbonization-pathways.md.
