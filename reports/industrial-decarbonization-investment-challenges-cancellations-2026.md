# Industrial Decarbonization Investment Challenges and Cancellations 2026

Recent data highlights significant headwinds in scaling industrial decarbonization projects. Between 2018 and Q3 2025, U.S. companies canceled $17 billion in projects across hydrogen, clean fuels, carbon management, and green cement—exceeding the $15 billion invested in new developments over the same period. [https://www.latitudemedia.com/news/industrial-decarbonization-investments-are-struggling/]

Hydrogen projects (green and blue) account for the largest share of cancellations, with $7 billion scrapped, including some already under construction. This reflects challenges in technology maturity, high capital costs, policy uncertainty, and supply chain issues.

The Clean Investment Monitor (Rhodium Group and MIT) tracks these trends, underscoring the need for stronger de-risking mechanisms, blended finance, and targeted incentives to improve project bankability.

## Key Implications
- **Risk of stranded assets and lost momentum**: Cancellations slow deployment in hard-to-abate sectors.
- **Opportunities for policy response**: Enhanced loan guarantees, carbon contracts for difference, and offtake agreements could reverse trends.
- **Sector focus**: Prioritize viable pathways like electrification where grid access is strong.

*Source: Clean Investment Monitor data via Latitude Media analysis (2025 data release).*
