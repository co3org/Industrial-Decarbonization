# Industrial Decarbonization Investment Challenges and Cancellations 2026

Recent analyses highlight significant headwinds for industrial decarbonization projects, with cancellations outpacing successes in several segments. Between 2018 and Q3 2025, U.S. industrial decarbonization investments faced mounting failures, driven by policy uncertainty, high costs, and market disruptions. [https://www.latitudemedia.com/news/industrial-decarbonization-investments-are-struggling/]

In Q1 2026, cancellations in energy and industry reached $11 billion, an 8% increase from the prior quarter and the third-highest on record since 2018 tracking began. Clean electricity projects accounted for 95% of these cancellations. [https://www.cleaninvestmentmonitor.org/reports/us-q1-2026-update]

Key factors include technology risks for first-of-a-kind projects, supply chain issues, and weakening policy signals. Reports emphasize the need for better risk-sharing mechanisms, such as specialized guarantee facilities, to address financing failures. [https://ccsi.columbia.edu/news/a-specialized-guarantee-facility-for-industrial-decarbonization-the-case-for-a-dedicated-pooled-risk-sharing-instrument/]

This overview complements existing financing enablers and market outlook reports by focusing on barriers and recent project outcomes.
