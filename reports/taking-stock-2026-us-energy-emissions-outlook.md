# Taking Stock 2026: US Energy and Emissions Outlook

**Source**: Rhodium Group, July 29, 2026. [https://rhg.com/research/taking-stock-2026/](https://rhg.com/research/taking-stock-2026/)

## Key Findings on Industrial Decarbonization

Industrial emissions in the US are projected to remain largely flat or see modest changes depending on economic growth and fossil fuel production scenarios. In low and mid-emissions cases, industrial emissions (including manufacturing, oil & gas, mining) show a 4% decrease or 1% increase by 2040 relative to 2025 levels. In a high-emissions scenario, they rise 15% by 2040. [https://rhg.com/research/taking-stock-2026/](https://rhg.com/research/taking-stock-2026/)

Oil and gas sector emissions, comprising nearly half of total industrial emissions, are a primary driver of divergence across scenarios.

The report emphasizes that outcomes hinge on fossil production volumes and broader economic fundamentals, highlighting risks and opportunities for industrial decarbonization pathways.
