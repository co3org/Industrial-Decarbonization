# Mission Possible Partnership Clean Industry Rising Report 2026

## Overview
The fifth edition of the Global Project Tracker from the Mission Possible Partnership highlights accelerating clean industry investment despite global volatility. As of mid-2026, 161 landmark clean industrial projects are in operation or under construction, with over 800 more in development worldwide. [Mission Possible Partnership Clean Industry Rising](https://missionpossiblepartnership.org/clean-industry-rising-interactive-report/)

Nineteen new Final Investment Decisions (FIDs) were recorded in the six months prior to June 2026—more than double the prior period—positioning 2026 as potentially the strongest year yet for clean industry investment. New value chains are emerging, estimated at $4.7 trillion.

## Key Findings
- **China leads**: Accounts for 45% of recent FIDs and over 60% over the past two years, driven by coordinated industrial strategy and scale-up of value chains.
- **India pipeline growth**: Up 30% in six months.
- **Cross-border alliances**: Strengthening supply chain resilience through strategic positioning.
- **Sector momentum**: Heavy industry projects in steel, cement, chemicals, and refining advancing via hydrogen, CCUS, and electrification pathways.

## Implications for Industrial Decarbonization
This report underscores the shift from policy announcements to concrete project execution, complementing existing coverage of sector pathways (e.g., steel, cement) and policy trackers in the repository. It emphasizes the role of strategic national approaches in building competitive clean industrial economies.

*Source: Mission Possible Partnership, June 2026.*
