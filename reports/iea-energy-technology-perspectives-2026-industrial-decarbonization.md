# IEA Energy Technology Perspectives 2026: Industrial Decarbonization Focus

The IEA's Energy Technology Perspectives (ETP) 2026 provides a comprehensive analysis of clean energy technology deployment, supply chain vulnerabilities, and industrial competitiveness, with dedicated insights into demand-side dynamics for industrial sectors. [https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-technology-perspectives-2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-technology-perspectives-2026)

## Key Industrial Decarbonization Insights

- Emphasis on supply chain resilience for clean technologies critical to industry, including manufacturing capacity and trade flows for electrolysers, heat pumps, and CCUS components.
- Analysis of industrial policy impacts on manufacturing costs and competitiveness in hard-to-abate sectors.
- Scenarios highlighting the need for accelerated deployment of electrification, hydrogen, and CCUS to meet net-zero goals, building on prior IEA roadmaps already covered in this repo.
- Focus on vulnerabilities in energy technology supply chains affecting industrial transitions.

This report complements existing repo content such as the IEA Industrial Decarbonization Roadmap 2026 and sector-specific pathways by providing updated 2026 modeling and policy recommendations on technology supply and demand.
