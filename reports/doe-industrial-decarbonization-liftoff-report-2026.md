# DOE Industrial Decarbonization Liftoff Report (2026)

The U.S. Department of Energy's Industrial Decarbonization Liftoff Report outlines pathways and investment needs for achieving net-zero emissions in the industrial sector by 2050, estimating $700 billion to $1 trillion in required public and private investment.

Key highlights include:
- Focus on hard-to-abate sectors such as steel, cement, chemicals, and refining.
- Emphasis on technologies like carbon capture, hydrogen, and electrification.
- Role of incentives from the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law and Inflation Reduction Act.
- Support for projects via the Loan Programs Office (LPO) and other programs.

[Source: DOE LPO page on Industrial Decarbonization Projects](https://www.energy.gov/edf/industrial-decarbonization-projects)
