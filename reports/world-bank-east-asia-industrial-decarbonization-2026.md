# World Bank Report: Industrial Decarbonization in East Asia (2026)

## Overview
The World Bank's August 2025 report (released publicly in 2026 context) is the first systematic assessment of industrial decarbonization pathways in East Asia, focusing on China, Indonesia, and Viet Nam. It identifies viable technical pathways, implementation challenges, and a comprehensive policy package to accelerate the transition to net-zero industry. [World Bank, 2026](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099062025065026929)

## Key Findings
- East Asia's industrial sector is a major global emitter; transforming energy, finance, technology, and jobs is essential.
- Viable pathways include electrification, hydrogen, CCUS, and material efficiency tailored to regional contexts.
- Challenges: high capital needs, technology access, and policy coordination across diverse economies.
- Policy recommendations emphasize blended finance, international cooperation, and targeted incentives for clean tech manufacturing.

## Relevance to Repository
This complements existing China, Vietnam, and India policy files by providing a regional synthesis and actionable insights for hard-to-abate sectors in one of the world's fastest-growing industrial regions.
