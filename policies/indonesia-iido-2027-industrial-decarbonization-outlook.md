# Indonesia Industrial Decarbonization Outlook (IIDO) 2027

*Image source: Pexels*

## Overview

Launched on 8 October 2026 by the Institute for Essential Services Reform (IESR), the Indonesia Industrial Decarbonization Outlook (IIDO) 2027 analyzes pathways for decarbonizing Indonesia's steel, cement, and other heavy industries amid rising global carbon border measures like the EU CBAM. [IESR, 2026](https://iesr.or.id/en/strengthening-policies-and-financing-to-drive-the-adoption-of-industrial-decarbonization-technologies/)

The report highlights how carbon pricing and CBAM will erode Indonesia's steel export competitiveness unless emissions intensity is reduced. Indonesian hot-rolled coil steel emits ~8.23 tCO₂e per tonne versus ~4.28 tCO₂e in India. [IESR, 2026](https://iesr.or.id/en/strengthening-policies-and-financing-to-drive-the-adoption-of-industrial-decarbonization-technologies/)

## Key Findings and Recommendations

- Existing policies (Green Industry Standards, energy/carbon pricing, emissions reporting) provide a foundation but require stronger incentives and financing.
- Immediate actions include energy efficiency, cleaner process heat, and industrial electrification.
- Carbon costs from CBAM starting 2026 make decarbonization urgent for export competitiveness.
- Government preparing financing incentives for green technology transitions. [IESR, 2026](https://iesr.or.id/en/strengthening-policies-and-financing-to-drive-the-adoption-of-industrial-decarbonization-technologies/)
