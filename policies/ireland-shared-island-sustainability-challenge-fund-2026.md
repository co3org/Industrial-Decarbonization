# Ireland Shared Island Sustainability Challenge Fund €26M for Industrial Decarbonization (2026)

On 12 October 2026, applications opened for the €26 million Shared Island Sustainability Challenge Fund, supporting novel decarbonisation technologies at operating manufacturing and industrial sites across Ireland and Northern Ireland. [industrialnews.co.uk, Oct 2026](https://industrialnews.co.uk/e26m-industrial-sustainability-fund-opens-for-applications/)

The fund allocates €13 million to each jurisdiction under the Shared Island Enterprise scheme, with €10 million from the Irish Government’s Shared Island Fund plus contributions from Invest Northern Ireland (€3M) and Enterprise Ireland (€3M). It targets demonstration projects implementing new decarbonisation technologies rather than general efficiency advice.

### Key Details
- **Northern Ireland**: Applications via Invest NI; window from noon 12 Oct to noon 13 Nov 2026 (may close early).
- **Ireland**: Separate route via Enterprise Ireland.
- Focus: Site-specific novel technologies for industrial decarbonisation.

This complements broader EU and national efforts, providing targeted support for hard-to-abate sectors in the region.
