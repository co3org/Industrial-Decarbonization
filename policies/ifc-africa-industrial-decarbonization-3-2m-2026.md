# IFC $3.2 Million Advisory Project for Industrial Decarbonization Across Africa (2026)

The International Finance Corporation (IFC), the private-sector arm of the World Bank Group, has launched a $3.2 million advisory project to accelerate industrial decarbonization in emerging markets, with a primary focus on Africa’s manufacturing, agribusiness, and services sectors. [IFC disclosure, Oct 2026](https://shore.africa/2026/10/08/ifc-commits-3-2m-to-africa/)

Approved in April 2025 and running through June 2028, the initiative targets hard-to-abate industrial sectors by helping private companies identify, develop, and implement commercially viable investments that reduce greenhouse gas emissions while improving energy and resource efficiency.

## Key Focus Areas
- Industrial electrification
- Renewable energy integration
- Waste heat recovery
- Carbon management
- Digital technologies for environmental performance

The project provides technical expertise, analytical support, and advisory services, including greenhouse gas assessments, decarbonization measure evaluations, and monitoring/reporting systems for climate-related investments.

This complements existing CIF and World Bank efforts in other regions (e.g., Brazil, Türkiye) by extending support to African manufacturing and agribusiness value chains.
