# Singapore Industrial Decarbonization Research Funding 2026

Singapore has pledged S$143 million (approximately $112 million USD) in research and development funding to support manufacturers in reducing emissions across industrial processes.

This initiative targets hard-to-abate sectors and aligns with broader efforts to advance low-carbon technologies in Asia's manufacturing hub.

[Carbon Herald, October 2026](https://carbonherald.com/capture/carbon-utilization/)

## Key Focus Areas
- Process efficiency and electrification
- Carbon capture and utilization
- Alternative fuels and hydrogen integration

The funding aims to accelerate deployment of decarbonization solutions while maintaining industrial competitiveness.
