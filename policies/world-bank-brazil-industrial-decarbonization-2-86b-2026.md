# World Bank $2.86B Brazil Industry Decarbonization Financing 2026

The World Bank Group is backing a $2.86 billion financing effort to decarbonize Brazil’s energy-intensive industries, including steel, cement, chemicals and aluminum. [https://esgnews.com/world-bank-backs-2-86b-brazil-industry-decarbonization/]

The package combines $1.06 billion in World Bank and Clean Technology Fund loans with an expected $1.8 billion in additional development and commercial financing.

Brazil aims to cut the greenhouse gas intensity of industrial GDP by 30% by 2033 while building competitive markets for low-carbon fuels and industrial materials.

Financing will focus on three areas:
- Low-carbon industrial commodities: emissions reductions across cement, steel, glass, chemicals and aluminum production.
- Low-carbon fuels: sustainable aviation fuel, e-methanol, biomethane and other emerging alternatives.
- Supporting policy and market development for low-carbon industrial materials.
