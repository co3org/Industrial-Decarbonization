# US DOE $43M+ Allocation for Industrial Decarbonization Technologies (August 2026)

**Source**: [DOE announcement via Facilities Dive, August 25, 2026](https://www.facilitiesdive.com/news/43m-allocated-industrial-decarbonization-technologies-doe/729483/)

The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) announced approximately $43.2 million in funding for 21 projects focused on advancing technologies to reduce energy use and greenhouse gas emissions in industrial subsectors.

- $38 million for 16 projects targeting cross-sector technologies.
- $5.2 million for 5 projects in partnership with the Electrified Processes for Industry without Carbon Institute.

**Focus Areas**:
- Electrification of industrial heat.
- Efficient energy use in industrial systems.
- Decarbonization of organic wastewater and wet waste treatment.

These investments address technologies not yet fully supported under the Inflation Reduction Act, with over 60% of industrial carbon reductions expected from such innovations. Projects have implications for industrial and commercial building operators.
