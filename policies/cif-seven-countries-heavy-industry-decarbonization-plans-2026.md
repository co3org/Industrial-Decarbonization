# CIF Seven Countries Developing Investment Plans for Heavy Industry Decarbonization (2026)

The Climate Investment Funds (CIF) announced in July 2026 that seven countries are developing investment plans to decarbonize heavy industry sectors including cement, steel, and chemicals. [https://www.cif.org/news/seven-countries-developing-investment-plans-decarbonize-heavy-industry](https://www.cif.org/news/seven-countries-developing-investment-plans-decarbonize-heavy-industry)

Mexico is focusing on cement, steel, and methanol value chains alongside green supply chains tied to nearshoring. Other participating countries are advancing similar blended finance and private capital mobilization strategies for industrial decarbonization.

This builds on existing CIF programs in the repository and highlights emerging multilateral efforts in 2026.
