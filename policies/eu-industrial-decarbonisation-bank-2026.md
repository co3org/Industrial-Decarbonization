# EU Industrial Decarbonisation Bank (IDB) 2026

The European Commission announced the €100 billion Industrial Decarbonisation Bank (IDB) as part of the Clean Industrial Deal to scale up low-carbon investments in hard-to-abate sectors.

## Key Features
- Endowment of €100bn to support industrial decarbonization projects.
- Focus on moving projects from funding awards to operational deployment.

## Recent Developments
A July 2026 report by the Clean Air Task Force (CATF) emphasizes that the IDB's success depends on practical design to deliver operating projects, not just funding awards. The report provides a blueprint ahead of the Commission's expected proposal. [CATF Report](https://www.catf.us/2026/07/designing-a-100bn-industrial-decarbonisation-bank-to-deliver-operating-projects/)
