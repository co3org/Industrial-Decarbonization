# US DOE $117M for Critical Minerals and Energy Innovation Supporting Industrial Decarbonization (August 2026)

The U.S. Department of Energy’s Office of Critical Minerals and Energy Innovation (CMEI) awarded $117 million to 56 projects on August 31, 2026, to strengthen American industries through energy and cost-saving innovations that support decarbonization pathways.

## Key Focus Areas
- Induction smelting of DRI-processed low-grade ores for steelmaking (University of Minnesota Duluth project enables continuous processing for productivity gains).
- Low-cost advanced cement materials from abundant raw materials (Solid Carbon, Inc. project uses belite-rich calcium sulfoaluminate and super sulfated slag cements from waste streams).
- Nitrogen fixation for fertilizers (Nitricity, Inc. plasma reactor technology as alternative to Haber-Bosch, reducing nitrous oxide pollution).

These projects target high-temperature heating, wastewater treatment, and supply chain security for steel, cement, chemicals, and related sectors central to industrial decarbonization.
