# Indonesia Pertamina-Jababeka Biomethane and Hydrogen Industrial Decarbonization Partnership 2026

State-owned energy company Pertamina and industrial estate developer Jababeka (KIJA) are expanding cooperation on clean energy supplies, including biomethane, solar power, and hydrogen, to support decarbonization in one of Indonesia’s largest manufacturing hubs at the Jababeka Industrial Estate in Cikarang, Bekasi. [https://jakartaglobe.id/business/pertamina-jababeka-plan-biomethane-and-hydrogen-expansion-for-industrial-decarbonization](https://jakartaglobe.id/business/pertamina-jababeka-plan-biomethane-and-hydrogen-expansion-for-industrial-decarbonization)

The partnership, initiated in 2022, now focuses on providing green energy to industrial tenants, exploring waste-to-energy solutions and hydrogen-powered transportation from 2027 onward. As of September 2026, 23 companies in the estate have installed rooftop solar with 14.5 MWp combined capacity.

This initiative targets hard-to-abate industrial emissions through fuel switching and renewable integration in manufacturing clusters.
