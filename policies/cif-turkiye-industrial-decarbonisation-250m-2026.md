# CIF $250 Million Türkiye Industrial Decarbonisation Programme (2026)

## Overview
The Climate Investment Funds (CIF) approved $250 million in catalytic funding on October 3, 2026, to accelerate Türkiye’s industrial decarbonisation efforts. The programme is expected to mobilise a total of $2.8 billion in investment, including $1.93 billion from CIF’s multilateral development bank (MDB) partners and $870 million from private investors and other sources. Türkiye’s Ministry of Treasury and Finance and Ministry of Industry and Technology developed the investment plan. It aligns with the country’s 2025 Climate Law and its pilot national Emissions Trading System. Implementation will occur through MDB partners (Asian Development Bank, European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, and World Bank Group/IFC) and the Türkiye Industrial Decarbonisation Investment Platform (TIDIP). The platform targets up to approximately $5.77 billion in investment for industrial decarbonisation by 2030.

## Key Focus Areas
- Concessional financing to de-risk private investment in hard-to-abate sectors.
- Support for energy efficiency, electrification, renewable integration, and low-carbon technologies in energy-intensive industries.
- Mobilisation of co-financing from MDBs and private sector to scale projects.

## Significance
This marks a major expansion of CIF’s support for industrial decarbonisation in emerging markets, building on similar programmes in Brazil, Mexico, and other regions. It provides a model for blending public concessional capital with private investment to drive industrial transformation while supporting Türkiye’s climate and economic goals.
