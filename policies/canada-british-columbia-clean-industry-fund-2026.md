# British Columbia Clean Industry Fund Industrial Decarbonization Projects 2026

British Columbia is allocating nearly $27 million through its Clean Industry Fund to support 17 projects aimed at cutting industrial emissions, improving efficiency, and supporting jobs across the province. The funding spans both the 2025 intake and a set of 2026 technology deployment projects.

## Highlights
- The 2025 intake allocates over $23 million for four Innovation Accelerator projects and $3 million for 13 Feasibility Studies projects.
- Funded projects target industrial decarbonization across regions including Vancouver Island, the Lower Mainland, the Interior, the Kootenays, and northeastern B.C.
- Innovation Accelerator support focuses on demonstration, piloting, and deployment of technologies that reduce industrial emissions.
- 2026 projects include carbon-capture technologies, emissions monitoring systems, advanced manufacturing processes, and energy-efficiency improvements.

## Key Focus Areas
- Carbon capture and emissions monitoring
- Electrification and fuel switching
- Energy-efficiency improvements

This initiative pairs industrial competitiveness with lower-carbon production, complementing existing Canadian efforts such as Alberta's industrial carbon pricing. [Source](https://tradersunion.com/news/financial-news/show/3421167-bc-clean-industry-fund-decarbonization-projects/)
