# IEA Industrial Decarbonization Roadmap 2026

The IEA's Industrial Decarbonization Roadmap outlines a pathway to achieve a 93% reduction in industrial emissions by 2050 through three phases focused on efficiency, fuel switching, and carbon capture. [Source](https://www.iea.org/policies/30337-industrial-decarbonization-roadmap)

Key elements include policy instruments for heavy industry sectors like steel, cement, and chemicals, building on the Policy Toolbox for Industrial Decarbonisation.
