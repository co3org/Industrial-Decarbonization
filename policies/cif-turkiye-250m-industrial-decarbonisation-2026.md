# CIF $250M Türkiye Industrial Decarbonisation Programme 2026

The Climate Investment Funds (CIF) governing board endorsed $250 million in catalytic funding for Türkiye’s industrial decarbonization investment plan on September 30, 2026. The programme is expected to mobilize a total of $2.8 billion, including $1.93 billion from CIF multilateral development bank (MDB) partners (Asian Development Bank, European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, World Bank Group/IFC) and $870 million from private sector and other sources. [https://www.cif.org/news/250-million-endorsed-turkiyes-industrial-decarbonization-mobilizing-28-billion]

## Key Objectives and Focus Areas
- Target hard-to-abate industrial sectors through green hydrogen, carbon capture, utilisation and storage (CCUS), energy efficiency, circular economy, renewable electricity deployment, and climate technologies/green markets acceleration.
- Support Türkiye’s 2053 net-zero goal by reducing or avoiding 39 million tonnes of CO₂ equivalent annually by 2035.
- Improve access to green finance for an estimated 378 businesses by 2035, with emphasis on small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and women-led enterprises.
- Coordinated via the Türkiye Industrial Decarbonization Investment Platform (TIDIP).

## Expected Impact
- Annual abatement of approximately 2.2 million tonnes of CO₂ equivalent by 2035, with lifetime reductions exceeding 39 million tonnes through 2053.
- Annual energy savings of 4,194 GWh.
- Leverage ratio of approximately 1:11.2 (every CIF dollar mobilizes over $11 in co-financing).

## Implementation
The plan was developed by Türkiye’s Ministry of Treasury and Finance and Ministry of Industry and Technology. It aligns with the country’s 2025 Climate Law and pilot national Emissions Trading System. Funding will be delivered through MDB partners with a focus on concessional finance to de-risk private investment.
