# Alliance for Industry Decarbonisation (AFID)

*Image source: Public domain / organizational materials*

The Alliance for Industry Decarbonisation (AFID) is a global collaborative platform led by the International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA) to accelerate practical and scalable solutions for reducing emissions in energy-intensive industries.

## Overview

AFID brings together industry leaders, technology companies, energy firms, and knowledge partners to advance decarbonization across heavy industry sectors including steel, chemicals, cement, and building materials. It emphasizes collaboration on technology deployment, policy alignment, and investment mobilization.

EMSTEEL Group (ADX: EMSTEEL), a major UAE-based steel and building materials manufacturer, was reappointed as Co-Chair of AFID for a second term in October 2026 during New York Climate Week. EMSTEEL serves alongside BASF, the world's largest chemical producer. This reappointment underscores the UAE's growing leadership in global industrial decarbonization efforts.

## Key Focus Areas

- Developing scalable low-carbon technologies and processes
- Enhancing industrial competitiveness while cutting emissions
- Facilitating knowledge sharing and partnerships across regions
- Supporting policy frameworks for clean industry transitions

## Significance

AFID addresses gaps in international coordination for hard-to-abate sectors, complementing national policies and financing initiatives tracked elsewhere in this repository. EMSTEEL's role highlights emerging leadership from the Middle East in steel and materials decarbonization pathways.
