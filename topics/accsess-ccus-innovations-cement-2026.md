# ACCSESS Project: CCUS Innovations for Cement and Hard-to-Abate Industries (2026)

The EU-funded ACCSESS project (2021–2026), led by SINTEF, concluded in August 2026 with three key innovations advancing carbon capture, utilisation, and storage (CCUS) for energy-intensive sectors including cement, pulp and paper, waste-to-energy, and biorefineries. [SINTEF, Oct 2026](https://www.sintef.no/en/latest-news/2026/three-innovations-three-steps-closer-to-industrial-decarbonisation/)

## Key Innovations

1. **Improved CO₂ Capture Technology**: Piloted an enhanced capture system (using rotating packed bed absorbers from Prospin/Proceler and Saipem solutions) achieving ~90% capture rates and capturing nearly 2 tonnes of CO₂ per day in operational tests at Heidelberg Materials' cement plant in Poland. Advanced from TRL 4–5 to TRL 7, enabling more efficient retrofits. [SINTEF, Oct 2026](https://www.sintef.no/en/latest-news/2026/three-innovations-three-steps-closer-to-industrial-decarbonisation/)

2. **CO₂ Utilisation for Precipitated Calcium Carbonate (PCC)**: Demonstrated mixing captured CO₂ with industrial mineral residues to produce valuable PCC, advancing circular economy approaches. Reached TRL 7. [SINTEF, Oct 2026](https://www.sintef.no/en/latest-news/2026/three-innovations-three-steps-closer-to-industrial-decarbonisation/)

3. **Integrated Cement Kiln with CCUS**: Developed a novel kiln design integrating CO₂ capture directly into production while utilising waste heat. Early-stage concept with significant future potential identified by partners. [SINTEF, Oct 2026](https://www.sintef.no/en/latest-news/2026/three-innovations-three-steps-closer-to-industrial-decarbonisation/)

## Relevance to Industrial Decarbonization

These advancements support cost-effective CCUS deployment in hard-to-abate sectors, aligning with broader efforts in cement decarbonization pathways and carbon capture in steel and cement. The project involved partners including Heidelberg Materials, StoraEnso, and others across Europe. [SINTEF Project Page](https://www.sintef.no/en/projects/2021/accsess/)

Further details and full reports are available via the project consortium.
