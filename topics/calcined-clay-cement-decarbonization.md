# Calcined Clay as a Low-Cost Pathway for Cement Decarbonization (2026)

Calcined clay offers cement producers a commercially proven, relatively low-cost route to reduce emissions by substituting clinker, the most emissions-intensive component of cement. Projects are now announced on every continent, according to analysis by the Leadership Group for Industry Transition (LeadIT). [https://carbon-pulse.com/category/net-zero-transition/industrial-decarbonisation/]

## Status Quo and Emissions Challenge
Cement production accounts for ~7-8% of global CO2 emissions, with ~60% from process emissions during limestone calcination. Traditional clinker production is energy- and carbon-intensive.

## Calcined Clay Technology
Calcined clay (metakaolin or similar) is produced by heating suitable clays to 600-800°C, far lower than clinker temperatures (~1450°C). It serves as a supplementary cementitious material (SCM) that can replace 20-50% of clinker in blended cements while maintaining performance.

### Key Benefits
- **Emissions reduction**: Up to 40% lower CO2 per tonne of cement compared to ordinary Portland cement.
- **Cost advantage**: Lower energy use and abundant clay resources in many regions.
- **Scalability**: Proven at commercial scale with minimal process changes.

## Global Project Pipeline
- Multiple announcements across Americas, Asia, Europe, and Africa.
- Integration with existing plants reduces capex barriers.

## Policy and Market Drivers
Supportive policies in the EU, India, and elsewhere incentivize SCM adoption alongside CCUS. Calcined clay complements other pathways like those in [topics/ccus-cement-decarbonization.md](topics/ccus-cement-decarbonization.md).
