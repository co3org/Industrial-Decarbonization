# Global Carbon Pricing Coalition for Heavy Industry Decarbonization (2026)

## Overview

Economists have proposed a coalition of countries implementing a shared carbon price floor targeting heavy industries (steel, cement, aluminum, and nitrogen fertilizer), which account for roughly 20% of global GHG emissions but less than 5% of economic output. This approach could reduce emissions by approximately 830 million tons annually while generating nearly $200 billion in public revenue each year.

The proposal addresses carbon leakage risks by creating a level playing field, with provisions for lower-income countries including subsidized financing for decarbonization. It builds on existing systems like the EU ETS and CBAM, with the Open Coalition on Compliance Carbon Markets (proposed at COP29 and endorsed by Brazil, EU, and China) as a potential forum for rules.

[Source: Earth.com summary of Science publication](https://www.earth.com/climate/carbon-price-floor-heavy-industry/)

## Key Elements

- **Target sectors**: Iron/steel, aluminum, cement, nitrogen fertilizer.
- **Mechanism**: Coordinated carbon price floor across participating countries.
- **Benefits**: 1.5% cut in global GHG emissions; revenue for clean tech incentives.
- **Incentives for participation**: Subsidized financing for developing nations.
