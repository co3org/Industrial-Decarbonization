# Supply Chain Industrial Heat Decarbonization Blueprint 2026

AstraZeneca, in partnership with ERM and Secaro, launched the Clean Heat Program to address industrial process heat decarbonization across complex supply chains. Industrial process heat is a dominant Scope 1 emissions driver in sectors like pharmaceuticals, with early data indicating ~80% of facility emissions are Scope 1 and ~60% from natural gas. Adoption of renewable direct heat remains below 10% of sites, projected to reach only ~15% by 2028 without coordinated action.

The program focuses on bridging gaps in heat decarbonization through planning, funding, and project bundling. It leverages an EU-based decarbonization fund and off-balance-sheet partners to overcome long paybacks and fragmented scopes. [AstraZeneca Clean Heat Program announcement](https://www.pharmaceuticalcommerce.com/view/astrazeneca-and-partners-debut-blueprint-for-decarbonizing-industrial-heat-in-supply-chains).

This complements existing coverage of industrial electrification and process heat pathways by emphasizing supply-chain buyer-supplier coordination and financing mechanisms.
