# Industrial Electrification and Process Heat Decarbonization Pathways

Electrification of industrial processes, particularly for process heat, represents a critical pathway for reducing emissions in hard-to-abate sectors. Recent analyses show that electrifying industrial heat in India is already cheaper than biomass, natural gas, or petroleum across all temperatures and cheaper than coal in bands covering 55% of needs.

## Key Technologies and Approaches
- Grid-interactive heat pumps and thermal storage for low-to-medium temperature heat.
- Direct electrification and advanced technologies for high-temperature applications (up to 1,800°C).
- Integration with renewables, biomass synergies, and industrial clusters for optimized systems.

## Regional Opportunities
- **India**: Electrification offers immediate cost advantages; see [Electrifying Industrial Heat in India](https://energyinnovation.org/report/electrifying-industrial-heat-in-india/).
- **Southeast Asia and global parks**: Biomass steam export combined with heat pumps enables zero-carbon heat supply, as demonstrated in optimized industrial park configurations avoiding millions of tons of CO2 annually.

## Policy and Market Enablers
Supportive frameworks include incentives for clean electricity, R&D for high-temp solutions, and cluster-based deployment. Emerging reports highlight electrification alongside CCUS, hydrogen, and efficiency as core building blocks.

## Challenges and Outlook
Barriers include grid infrastructure, electricity pricing, and technology maturity for extreme temperatures. 2026 analyses project significant market growth driven by these solutions.
