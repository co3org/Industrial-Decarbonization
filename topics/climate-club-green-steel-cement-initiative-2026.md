# Climate Club Green Steel and Cement Targets Initiative (2026)

The Germany-initiated Climate Club is advancing international cooperation to scale near-zero and low-emissions steel and cement production. At COP 30 in Belém, Brazil (October 2026), members endorsed a global pledge to grow markets for green industrial products, with potential quantitative targets for green steel and cement market share to be set by COP 31.

## Key Elements
- **Endorsing countries and organizations**: Australia, Brazil, Canada, Germany, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Kenya, Sweden, UK; African Development Bank, Industrial Transition Accelerator, Climate Investment Funds (CIF), Green Climate Fund, IRENA.
- **Focus areas**: Mobilizing investments, driving demand for green products, enhancing carbon accounting transparency, developing harmonized green standards.
- **Supporting actions**: Germany, UK, and Global Industry Hub committing €30 million to a new industry decarbonization hubs accelerator facilitated by UNIDO for emerging economies.

Green industrial products could be worth over $1 trillion by 2030. This builds on existing national policies and complements efforts in heavy industry decarbonization pathways.

Sources: [World Ports Organization COP coverage](https://www.worldports.org/cop-climate-club-eyes-green-steel-cement-targets/), [Argus Media reporting on COP 30 side event](https://www.argusmedia.com/).

This initiative addresses demand-side measures and international standards critical for scaling technologies in steel and cement sectors already covered in sector-specific topics.
