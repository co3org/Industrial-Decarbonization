# Pure Oxyfuel Technology for Cement Decarbonization (2026)

The CI4C (Cement Innovation for Climate) Pure Oxyfuel plant in Mergelstetten, Germany, was officially inaugurated in July 2026, marking a major milestone in industrial-scale demonstration of carbon capture for cement production. [thyssenkrupp Polysius](https://www.thyssenkrupp-decarbon-technologies.com/en/news) led the project in collaboration with the CI4C consortium (Buzzi/Dyckerhoff, Heidelberg Materials, SCHWENK, Vicat) under the catch4climate initiative.

## Technology Overview
Pure Oxyfuel replaces ambient air with pure oxygen in the clinker burning process, raising CO₂ concentration in exhaust gases to enable capture of up to 95% of emissions, including process emissions from limestone calcination. The plant has a capacity of ~450 tons of clinker per day and serves as an R&D platform for validation and scaling.

## Significance for Hard-to-Abate Sectors
This demonstration addresses one of cement's core challenges: unavoidable process CO₂. It complements existing pathways like calcined clay and CCUS covered elsewhere in the repository. The technology is now positioned for commercialization by thyssenkrupp Calvion.
